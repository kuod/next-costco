How the score works

Every Census place with 40,000+ residents and a warehouse at least 8 miles away (339 places) is scored 0–100 as a weighted sum of eight components. The default weights are shown below; use the sliders above to change them. The top 50 are chosen greedily so no two picks sit within 20 miles of each other, so the list isn't one suburb cluster repeated.

  • Default weights: distance to nearest Costco 22%, underserved catchment 18%, income 18%, education 9%, density 9%, metro saturation 9%, rival-club distance 10%, homeownership 5%.

Metro area is the Census metropolitan statistical area. Underserved catchment is the population living within 10 miles of the place in towns that are themselves more than 10 miles from any warehouse. Rival-club distance is the straight-line distance to the nearest Sam's Club or BJ's. A rival club nearby proves the market supports a warehouse club, but one right next door means a head-to-head fight, so the score peaks at 4–18 miles and falls off inside 4 and beyond 45. This is a judgment call: if you read rivals as pure competition, set its slider to 0. People per warehouse is metro population divided by warehouses in the metro.

Data & caveats

  • Demographics: American Community Survey 5-year estimates (latest release) via Census Reporter. Land area and coordinates: Census Gazetteer 2024. Metro delineations: OMB/Census July 2023.
  • Costco, Sam's Club and BJ's locations come from OpenStreetMap and may lag recent openings or announcements. A city that looks wide open may already have a warehouse under construction. Verify against costco.com before drawing conclusions.
  • Distances are straight-line from the Census internal point of each place, not drive time. Places whose water area is more than twice their land area are excluded because their centroids are unreliable.
  • Places are mapped to a metro through their nearest county centroid, which can misplace a few places near county lines.
  • This is a screening model. It doesn't know about land availability, zoning, traffic, competing warehouse clubs (Sam's Club, BJ's) or Costco's actual expansion plans.
